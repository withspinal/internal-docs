These pages describe how data in the Infor SX.e ERP maps onto the Spine. Read this page
first.

This is research. No adapter exists, and nothing here has been tested against a live
SX.e system.

## What SX.e is

SX.e is an ERP for wholesale distributors, also known by its older names Trend and
NxTrend SX.enterprise. Infor now sells it only as CloudSuite Distribution (CSD), hosted
by Infor, and these pages assume that. We read it through the Infor Data Lake, because
CSD gives no database access. Older customers still run SX.e on their own servers, and
these pages do not cover that.

## How SX.e is organised

A table is named after the screen that maintains it, and the first two letters are the
module: AR for customers and receivables, OE for sales orders, SA for system setup, GL
for the general ledger. Every table carries the company number, `cono`, a change
timestamp, `transdttmz`, and nine customer-defined fields, `user1` to `user9`.

These are the tables the mapping reads, grouped by the Spine objects they feed.

### Clients, ledgers, and business units

In Spine terms, a company is a client with its ledger, and a division is a business
unit.

| Table | What is in it |
|---|---|
| `sasc` | One row per company: name, tax ids, currency, fiscal calendar, and every administrator option, such as aging periods and write-off limits |
| `sastn` | Number-coded lists, including the divisions with their names and tax ids |
| `sastc` | Foreign currencies, with the exchange rate last entered for each. Empty for a single-currency company |
| `sasj` | Journals: one per posting batch, with the posting date behind every `gl_date` |
| `sasoo` | Operators, with the divisions each may see. The source for business unit grants |
| `smsn`, `icsd`, `crsb` | Salesreps, warehouses, and banks. Labels only |

### Accounts, sites, and contacts

One customer record is the party, the account, and the bill-to site. A ship-to is a
site with its own credit settings.

| Table | What is in it |
|---|---|
| `arsc` | The customer: name, address, terms, credit limit, order status, credit manager, stored aging, and payment statistics |
| `arss` | Ship-tos and jobs: an address with its own terms, credit limit, balances, and lien data |
| `arsg` | Customer master groups, with a shared credit limit and hold settings |
| `sasta` | Letter-coded lists: payment terms, customer types, ship vias, return reasons |
| `contacts`, `contacts-roles`, `contacts-methods` | People, their links to customers and ship-tos, and their emails and phone numbers |
| `email-links` | The addresses selected for each document type. The first rule for who to chase. Columns not documented |
| `notes` | Free-text notes on customers, ship-tos, transactions, and orders |

### Documents, installments, and lines

One table holds every AR row, and the type code says what each row is in Spine terms.

| Table | What is in it |
|---|---|
| `aret` | Every AR row. Types 0, 1, 4, 5, and 6 are documents, type 11 is an installment, type 7 is a receipt, and type 3 is a receipt's unapplied amount |
| `oeeh` | The sales order behind an invoice: currency, tax, dates, division, warehouse, and customer PO number |
| `oeel` | Order lines: product, quantity, price, net amount, and tax. Our `ar_transaction_lines` |
| `addon` | Order charges such as freight |
| `arett` | Sales tax per jurisdiction per order |
| `oeelcr` | The tie from a correction or return line to the original line. Not in the Data Lake |

### Receipts and applications

A receipt is a row in `aret`, and an application is the installment row it paid.

| Table | What is in it |
|---|---|
| `aret`, type 7 | Check records: one per payment. Our `receipts` |
| `aret`, type 11, closed | Paid installments, stamped with the amount, date, and cheque. Our `receipt_applications` |
| `aretp` | One record per payment, with a foreign key from each paid installment. Present only when payment reversals are switched on |

### Reconciliation

The customer record stores the aging we compute, and the ledger is only needed for the
tie-out.

| Table | What is in it |
|---|---|
| `arsc`, the balance fields | The five aging buckets, future, credits, unapplied cash, and COD, as of the last monthly roll |
| `arsp` | Twelve months of month-end aging per customer |
| `glsa`, `glet` | Accounts with a balance per period, and the postings behind them |
| `gletv` | Currency revaluations, with the old and new rate |
| `sasb` | A log of balance corrections made by hand |
| `oeehc` | Orders on credit hold. The nearest thing to a stored credit hold |
| `areta` | In Infor's table list with no description |

## How well SX.e fits

SX.e fits the Spine's documents well and its cash ledger badly. One table, `aret`, holds
every AR record, which matches `ar_transactions`. SX.e keeps no record of which payment
settled which invoice.

| Spine table | SX.e tables | Fit | Main issue |
|---|---|---|---|
| `clients`, `ledgers` | `sasc` | Good | A company is not always one legal entity |
| `business_units` | `sastn`, the division list | Good | The division can be blank |
| `customers`, `customer_accounts` | `arsc`, with terms from `sasta` | Good | Links between customers have no Spine column |
| `customer_sites`, `site_uses` | `arsc` for the bill-to, `arss` for ship-tos | Good | A ship-to has its own credit limit and balances |
| `contacts`, `contact_assignments` | `contacts`, `contacts-roles`, `contacts-methods`, and `arsc` for the AP contact | Partial | No role says who to chase |
| `customer_collection_profiles` | `arsc` | Good | Most columns have a source. None has a dunning setting that SX.e acts on |
| `ar_transactions` | `aret`, types 0, 1, 4, 5, 6 | Good | No stored open balance |
| `ar_transaction_lines`, `ar_transaction_tax_lines` | `oeeh`, `oeel`, `addon`, `arett` | Partial | Only invoices from a sales order have lines |
| `invoice_installments` | `aret`, type 11 | Partial | Rows are replaced when money moves |
| `receipts` | `aret`, type 7, and `aretp` | Partial | `aretp` exists only if payment reversals are on |
| `receipt_applications` | `aret`, the paid type 11 rows, linked through `sasj` | Poor | No application table. Reversals change rows in place |
| `ar_adjustments` | `aret`, the write-off on a paid row, and type 8 debit memos | Partial | No reason code and no approval step |
| `disputes` | `aret`, the dispute flag on a type 11 row | Poor | A flag with no amount, reason, or date |
| Reconciliation | `arsc` balances, `glsa`, `glet` | Good | Stored aging buckets are stale between monthly rolls |

## What to settle first

Settle these with the customer before design starts. The
[discovery questions](/erp/infor-sxe/discovery-questions) page has the full list.

1. How SX.e holds their legal entities: one company, legal entities as divisions, or
   several companies. It decides what a client is and whether customers are shared.
2. That they run CloudSuite Distribution, and who their Infor partner is.
