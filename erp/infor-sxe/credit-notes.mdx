In the Spine a credit note is an `ar_transactions` row of type `credit_memo` with
negative amounts, and applying it to an invoice is a `receipt_applications` row with
`receipt_id` NULL and `funding_source = credit_memo`. SX.e has two kinds of credit, and
they differ in whether that application exists from birth.

## Two kinds of credit_memo

SX.e splits what the Spine calls a credit memo into a credit that is applied at birth and a credit that waits on the account.

| SX.e record | Type code | In Spine terms |
|---|---|---|
| Credit memo (CM) | 6 | A `credit_memo` whose `open_amount` is always 0, plus one application written at the same moment, against the one invoice the CM names |
| Miscellaneous credit (MC) | 5 | A `credit_memo` with an open balance. Its applications come later, when someone applies it |

A CM can never exceed its invoice's remaining balance. If the credit is larger, SX.e
applies what fits as a CM and writes an MC for the rest.

Both carry negative `original_amount` in the Spine. Set the sign from the type code,
because we don't know whether SX.e stores credits negative.

## Where credits come from

Most credits start as a return order or a correction order in sales order entry. The
invoice suffix tells us which kind we got.

| Source | Becomes |
|---|---|
| A return or correction order that names an invoice, with **Auto Apply Credit to Original Invoice** on | A CM. The application already exists |
| A return or correction order with no invoice, or without auto apply | An MC |
| A sales order whose total is negative | An MC |
| A credit typed into AR, suffix 99 | A CM or an MC, as the clerk chose |

A credit from an order has `ar_transaction_lines`, read from `oeel` like an invoice with
the sign flipped. A credit typed into AR has no lines. Put the record and its origin in
`source_document_type`, such as `CM/RM` or `MC/AR`.

## Applying an MC writes applications

A clerk applies an MC in cash receipts entry with the posting type **Apply Credits**. One
MC can settle several invoices. In Spine terms, each invoice installment it settles gets
an application with `receipt_id` NULL and `funding_source = credit_memo`.

SX.e records no amount per credit and invoice pair. The only link is the posting journal
on the settled installment, which appears to be in `aret.crjrnlno` and `aret.crsetno`.
When one posting applies several credits to several invoices, the adapter has to split
the amounts itself.

SX.e has no function that unapplies a credit. A wrongly applied credit is corrected by
hand, and the correction looks like a new debit and a new credit.

## A partly applied MC is replaced, not reduced

In the Spine, applying part of a credit lowers its `open_amount`. SX.e closes the MC and
writes what is left as a new record. What that record is depends on the setting
**Resequence Unapplied Credits**.

| Setting | What is left | In Spine terms |
|---|---|---|
| On | New records that keep the MC's number with new sequence numbers | Key the `credit_memo` on the number without the sequence, and sum the active sequences for `open_amount` |
| Off, the default | One unapplied cash record for the leftover of every credit in the posting, with no link back | Treat the new record as a new `credit_memo`, and write an application with `transaction_id` NULL against each original credit for the amount that moved |

With the setting off, treating the leftover as cash would let credit value fulfil a
promise to pay, so it stays a credit.

## The application cannot name its credit

`receipt_applications` has no column for which credit memo funded it. The proposed
`source_transaction_id` is not built. For a CM this costs little, because a CM is
applied once to the invoice it names. For an MC it means a collector cannot confirm that
a promised credit landed on the disputed invoice. Until the column exists, put the
credit's key in the application's `external_system_identifier`.

## A refund is an invoice paid by a credit

To refund a credit balance, Infor's
[documented procedure](https://docs.infor.com/sxe/latest/en-us/sxolh/wdt1503926014849.html)
creates a manual invoice with suffix 99, applies the credit to it, and has accounts
payable send a cheque. In Spine terms it looks like a real `invoice` settled by a
`credit_memo`. It must not reach a collections run. It can only be recognised by its
general ledger account or reference text, which are the customer's own convention.

## Check on the data

These come from the tables once we have access.

| Question | Where to look |
|---|---|
| Is **Resequence Unapplied Credits** on? | What is left after a partial application. New sequence numbers under the credit's own number mean on. A single `0-00` unapplied cash row means off |
