The Spine proves a load by summing open installments and comparing the result with the
ERP's own figures. SX.e stores those figures on the customer record. They are our aging
buckets, kept as stored numbers instead of computed from installments.

## SX.e stores what we compute

The customer record carries a set of balance fields. In Spine terms they are the output
of our aging query, for one account, as of the last time SX.e ran its monthly roll.

| SX.e field | In Spine terms |
|---|---|
| `arsc.periodbal` | The five aging buckets |
| `arsc.futinvbal` | Installments due after the end of the current bucket |
| `arsc.servchgbal` | Open `interest_note` documents |
| `arsc.misccrbal`, `arsc.uncashbal` | Open credit memos and unapplied cash, stored positive |
| `arsc.codbal` | Open COD invoices. Outside the total by default |
| `arsc.ordbal` | Open orders. Not a receivable |

Infor documents the total as:

```text
total = periods 1 to 5 + future invoices + service charges
        - miscellaneous credits - unapplied cash
```

The transactions are the truth. When these fields disagree with the rows in `aret`,
SX.e's own reports correct the fields, never the rows.

## The total is current, the buckets are not

SX.e updates the total as transactions post, so it is right every night. The split
across buckets changes only when the Customer Entry Roll Balances Report (ARER) runs,
usually monthly. Between rolls a new document goes into period 1 whatever its due date.

So tie totals to the customer record, and tie buckets to a report run on the day.
`arsc.lastagedt` says when the buckets were last right.

The buckets themselves map one to one onto ours, with one offset to remember: period 1
is current, not 1 to 30 days past due. Periods 2 to 5 are past due. The lengths are in
`sasc.arperdays`, and some customers reset them every month.

## Two settings move credits into the buckets

**Age Miscellaneous Credits** and **Age Service Charges**, both off by default, move
those amounts inside the period buckets. When one is on, the amount is already in the
periods, and the equation above counts it twice. Ask which are on before picking an
equation.

## The tie-out

Ask the customer to run two reports as of the date of the load.

| Level | Spine side | SX.e side |
|---|---|---|
| Customer total | Open documents minus open credits and unapplied cash | The total balance equation |
| Buckets | Open installments aged by due date | Customer Period Analysis Report (ARRP), aged by due date |
| General ledger | Open amounts by `gl_date`, in ledger currency, per AR control account | Customer Trial Balance Report (ARRT) |

Start only when the customer's own data is clean. The Customer Balancing Audit Report
(ARRB) lists customers whose stored fields do not match their transactions.

On `ar_reconciliation_runs`, set `aging_basis` to `due_date`, since ARER has no other
option, and `bucket_edges` from `sasc.arperdays`.

## Differences that are not bugs

Each of these produces a variance that looks like a fault in the adapter.

| Cause | What to do |
|---|---|
| An aging setting put credits inside the periods | Pick the matching equation |
| New documents sit in period 1 until the next roll | Tie buckets to ARRP, not to `arsc.periodbal` |
| COD is outside the total | Tie it separately against `arsc.codbal` |
| An invoice has a document row and installment rows | Sum one kind. See [the open balance](/erp/infor-sxe/invoices#open_amount-is-computed) |
| Rebate rows never reach the general ledger | Leave out type code 2 |
| A clerk changed a balance field by hand | The change is logged in `sasb`. Report it as explained |

`arsc` has a second set of balance fields with the prefix `cust`, and each ship-to has a
set with the prefix `job`. Which set covers the customer alone and which includes its
ship-tos is not documented. Test it on the first load.

## Check on the data

These come from the tables once we have access.

| Question | Where to look |
|---|---|
| Are the two aging settings on? | Whether the stored total equals the default equation. Off by the credits or the service charges means a setting is on. The setting itself is in `sasc.arrollcd`, whose values are not documented |
| When did ARER last run? | `arsc.lastagedt` |

## Ask the customer

The data cannot answer these.

1. Which report is their AR balance of record.
2. On which days they run month end. A load taken in the middle will not tie.
