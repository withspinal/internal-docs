Use this page to run discovery with a customer on Infor SX.e. Ask the first question
before anything else. It decides what a Spine client is, and everything else keys on
that.

The SX.e administrator or the customer's Infor partner can answer most of these. Names
in bold are settings in SA Administrator Options.

## The most important question

How does SX.e hold your legal entities? SX.e users do not say "legal entity". They say
"company" and "division", so ask it in three parts, and ask the controller, not IT.

1. How many company numbers do you run?
2. What does a division mean in your business: a branch, a product line, or a separate
   legal entity with its own tax id?
3. Is the **Divisional** option on in SA Company Setup?

| Answer | Spine clients | Customers |
|---|---|---|
| One company, one legal entity | One | Shared. The simplest case |
| One company, legal entities as divisions | One per division | Shared |
| Several companies | One per company | Duplicated per company, with nothing to link them |

The second answer needs **Divisional** on, so that every AR row carries a division. A
division with its own tax id in the setup screen does not prove it is a legal entity.
Some distributors fill that in for branch reporting.

## Access

These decide whether we can read the data at all.

1. Do you run CloudSuite Distribution? These pages assume it.
2. Is **Replicate to Data Lake** on, and for which tables?
3. Who is your Infor partner?
4. Which AR, payment, or collections products already connect to SX.e?
5. Have you ever run the Change Customer Number report? It rewrites the customer
   number on every record.

## Practice

These are about how the customer works, which the data cannot tell us.

1. Is each division a legal entity or a branch? The controller knows.
2. How is the collections team organised: by division, branch, or credit manager?
3. Do collectors chase by job or by customer?
4. Which contact type means accounts payable, if the code descriptions do not say?
5. How do you refund a credit balance, and what marks the clearing invoice?
6. How do you correct a credit applied to the wrong invoice?
7. Should COD invoices be chased?
8. Which customer numbers stand for card processors or a lockbox dummy?
9. Which report is your AR balance of record, and on which days do you run month end?
10. How do you send statements, and through which product?
11. Who clears the dispute flag?
12. Where do collectors record calls and promises?

## Documents to ask for

Two reports give us the figures to tie to, and the dictionary names the fields Infor
does not document.

| Document | Why |
|---|---|
| Customer Trial Balance Report (ARRT), as of the date of our first load | The general ledger tie |
| Customer Period Analysis Report (ARRP), as of the same date, aged by due date | The bucket tie |
| The data dictionary from SX Dictionary Help, for `aret`, `aretp`, `arsc`, `arss`, and `email-links` | The meaning of fields we have only by name |

## What we check ourselves

Once we have access, these come from the tables. Each page ends with the same list for
its topic.

| Question | Where to look |
|---|---|
| Which company numbers are live? | Which `cono` values have recent rows in `aret` |
| Are divisions on AR rows, and do they carry tax ids? | Whether `aret.divno` is ever blank, and `sastn.fedtaxid` |
| Any foreign currency? | Rows in `sastc`, and non-blank `arsc.currencyty` |
| Do they use ship-tos as jobs? | `arss` rows with `jobdesc` or lien fields filled |
| Who receives statements? | `arsc.estmttype`, and `email-links` |
| Do they use **Statements to Customer**, **Invoice To**, or groups? | Non-blank `arsc.custno2`, `arsc.fpcustno`, `arsc.groupid` |
| Are credit rows stored negative? | The sign of `aret.amount` on type codes 5 and 6 |
| Which type codes exist? | `aret.transcd`, grouped with a count |
| Do any terms split an invoice? | Documents with more than one scheduled pay row |
| How are short payments recorded? | Type code 8 rows against closed rows with `aret.pifamt` set |
| Is **Resequence Unapplied Credits** on? | What is left after a partial credit application |
| Is **Allow AR Payment Reversals** on, and since when? | Rows in `aretp`, and the earliest `aretp.postdttz` |
| One check record per cheque, or per invoice paid? | Count type code 7 rows in a journal and set with several paid rows |
| How are ACH and wire receipts recorded? | Check records with `checkno` zero and `paymentrefer` filled |
| Which field says how a scheduled payment was closed? | `checknopaid`, `crjrnlno`, and `reversedfl` on closed rows |
| Does `paymtamt` include the discount? | Rows with `discamt` above zero |
| Are the aging settings on? | Whether the stored total matches the default equation |
| When did the monthly roll last run? | `arsc.lastagedt` |
| Do they use the dispute flag? | Rows with `aret.disputefl` set |

Pull these rows first. Each settles a question that documentation could not.

| Case | Tables |
|---|---|
| An invoice paid in part | `aret` |
| A cheque that paid two invoices and left a remainder, before and after reversal | `aret`, `aretp` |
| A credit memo from a return order | `aret`, `oeeh` |
| A partly applied miscellaneous credit | `aret` |
| A customer with ship-tos and contacts | `arsc`, `arss`, `contacts`, `contacts-roles` |
