This page is for an engineer who has to change or debug what happens when a person talks to
the agent about an AI reminder draft. After reading it you can follow one message from the
box in the draft panel to the rewritten draft, and you know which module owns each step.

The spec is `specs/november-2026/outreach-redraft-chat/spec.md` in `dashboard-backend`.

## Two agents, one conversation

Two agents are involved and their work does not overlap.

- The issue agent holds the conversation. Its code is in
  `src/services/issue_resolution/agent/`. It reads what the person wrote, calls tools, and
  raises cards.
- The reminder agent writes reminders. It is the `NaturalCampaignWorkflow` in
  `src/temporal/natural_campaign/`. It alone decides when a reminder goes, which invoices
  it chases and what it says.

The issue agent never writes or edits a reminder. Its tools pass the person's instruction
to the reminder agent, which reworks its own draft.

## Where the conversation comes from

A draft has an approval task, the task of its `AI_OUTREACH_APPROVAL` issue. The issue's
`context` holds `source_planned_action_id`, the id of the draft's planned action.

A conversation is keyed on a thread, and a draft for a customer who never emailed has no
thread. `POST /tasks/<task_id>/reminder-conversation` gives the task a thread id the first
time it is called and returns the conversation keyed on it. The draft panel calls it when
the person sends their first message.

When the reminder sends, it sends on that thread. `thread_of_draft` in
`src/services/natural_outreach/rework_record.py` finds it. The customer's reply then lands
in the same conversation.

A draft on a segment that sends without approval has no approval task. It has no
conversation, and its box calls the redraft endpoint directly.

## The tools

All five are in `src/services/issue_resolution/agent/tools/`.

| Tool | File | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `list_reminder_drafts` | `reminders.py` | Reads the customer's pending drafts at the desk. |
| `request_rework` | `reminders.py` | Hands a person's instruction for one draft to the reminder agent. No card. |
| `cancel_reminder` | `reminders.py` | Raises a question card whose choices are reasons. Picking a reason cancels the draft. |
| `remember` | `reminders.py` | Raises a question card asking who a preference is for. Picking a scope saves it. |
| `ask_person` | `ask.py` | Raises a question card with two to four choices that only answers the agent. |

`request_rework`, `cancel_reminder` and `remember` refuse unless a person wrote in the
current run. The customer and the desk always come from the run, never from what the
model wrote.

## A rework, step by step

A rework starts with the person's message and ends with the agent saying the new version
is ready. These are the steps between.

1. `request_rework` screens the instruction and calls `begin_rework` in
   `src/services/natural_outreach/rework.py`. The redraft endpoint calls the same function.
2. `begin_rework` sets the lease `redraft_requested_at` on the draft's `planned_outreach`
   row and writes `redraft_request`: the instruction, who asked, when, and the subject,
   body and send time as they stood. It signals the reminder workflow after commit.
3. The workflow rewrites the draft. `begin_redrafts_activity` refreshes the "as it stood"
   part of `redraft_request` where the rewrite starts.
4. `settle_redrafts_activity` calls `settle_reworks` in `rework_record.py`. For a draft that
   was rewritten it writes one `draft_feedback` row of kind `REPLACED`, holding the before
   and after. For any settled request it writes a `rework_settled` event into the
   conversation and wakes it. Then it clears `redraft_request`.
5. The issue agent runs again and tells the person the new version is ready, or that it
   could not be done.

The draft's read, `GET /planned-outreach/by-action/<action_id>/draft`, returns the kept
versions as `replaced_versions`. The panel draws each one greyed above the message that
asked for it.

One rework runs at a time on a draft. A second `request_rework` during the lease returns
"already under way" and signals nothing.

## The question card

A question is a card of kind `question` (`QuestionCardSummary` in
`src/services/agent_tool_requests/models.py`). It has two to four choices. A choice may be
marked `suggested`, and may carry `does`: cancel a draft, or remember a note for a scope.

A person answers by pressing a choice. That is `POST /tasks/<task_id>/cards/<card_id>/decision`
with `decision: "approve"` and `choice` set to the choice's key. "Not now" is a decline and
applies nothing. Typing "yes" never answers a question.

After the pick, the thread's session workflow runs `QuestionCardExecutor` in
`src/services/issue_resolution/card_send.py`. It does what the choice says through
`src/services/natural_outreach/draft_decisions.py`, in the name of the person who picked.
The agent's next run starts with what was picked and what came of it.

Only one card waits per conversation. A question and an email card never wait together,
and a second question replaces the first.

## Adding something a conversation can do about a reminder

Add a tool in `tools/reminders.py` that calls the reminder's own owner. Do not write a
draft's words, date or invoices from a tool. If the act needs a person's press, build its
choices with `question_card` and raise them with `ask` from `tools/ask.py`, then teach
`QuestionCardExecutor` what the choice does.

A new card kind or event kind is read by strict unions. Ship every reader one deploy
before the code that writes it, or a pod that does not know the kind fails on any
conversation that holds one.

## Where to look when it goes wrong

- The draft stays "Redrafting" for 30 minutes and then reads "Not rewritten". The lease
  expired with no settle. Look for `settle_redrafts_activity` and `settle_reworks` in the
  worker's logs for the customer account.
- The draft was rewritten and the agent said nothing. `settle_reworks` only tells a
  conversation that a person wrote in. Check that the approval task has a `thread_id` and
  that the conversation has a `PERSON_MESSAGE` row.
- A pick did nothing. Read the card's `outcome` and `outcome_detail.reason` in
  `agent_tool_requests`. A refusal says why in words.
