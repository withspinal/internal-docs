A collection desk is the container a collections team works in. Read this page before you
touch the desk tables, the desk settings pages, or anything that routes a customer email
or a chase to a person. After it you will know what a desk owns, what it does not, and
what has to exist before a group can create one.

## What a desk is

A desk is one shared inbound mailbox with a team, a set of business units, and the
addresses the team sends from. Customers write to the mailbox and collectors copy it, so
the mailbox is the desk's identity and its thread of record. A customer who owes several
business units on one desk is chased once, with one total itemised per unit. That roll-up
is the point of the desk: without it, a customer hears from Spinal once per business unit.

A desk is group-level configuration. It sits above the business unit and below the group,
and it is the unit of chasing and of inbox scope. Business-unit grants keep scoping the
other pages (customers, transactions, reporting). Inside a desk, membership is the only
scope.

## What a desk is made of

| Part | What it is | Where it comes from |
|------|------------|---------------------|
| Name | Shown to the team in the desk switcher. Customers never see it. | Typed once. Unique per group, case-insensitive. |
| Inbound mailbox | The shared mailbox the desk reads. Its identity and thread of record. | A connected shared mailbox, read through the group's Entra app. |
| Business units | The desk's book: the units it chases as one. | Ticked from the group's business units. A unit is on one desk at most. |
| Members | The people who can see the desk's inbox, each as a manager or a member. | Added by email. A row links to a user when that person is already admitted to the group. |
| Outbound channel | The address the desk sends from. | Picked from the group's send-enabled mailboxes only. One per desk for now: it is what agents send through. |
| Email branding | The header and footer letterhead on the desk's emails. | Set per business unit on the desk's Email branding page. |

Accounts, collectors and contacts are not on the list because nobody picks them. They
derive from the bill-to site uses in the desk's business units on the next ERP sync.
Agents, chase segments and the desk-scoped inbox are later milestones of the same Linear
project, "Collection desks". Phone numbers are not a channel yet.

A desk is created complete: name, inbound mailbox, outbound address, at least one
business unit and at least one member, in one call. It stays complete afterwards: the
last member cannot be removed and the outbound address cannot be cleared, only changed.

## Two rules the database enforces

Both were decided on 29 September 2026 and both are constraints, not conventions. Do not
relax them in application code and do not add a second path around them.

- **A desk and its inbound mailbox are one to one.** `collection_desks.inbound_shared_mailbox_connection_id`
  is NOT NULL and UNIQUE. A second desk on the same mailbox gets a 409 that names the desk
  already holding it, even when two admins race for it.
- **A business unit is on at most one desk.** `collection_desk_business_units` is UNIQUE on
  `business_unit_id` alone, not per desk. Ticking a unit that another desk holds gets a
  409 that names that desk.

A desk also needs at least one business unit, on create and on every later edit.

### Moving a unit to another desk

The second desk is usually built from units the first one holds, so a unit on another
desk can be ticked and moves; the move is always explicit and confirmed. In the picker a
unit on another desk you are on reads "On Ports" and, once ticked, "Moving from Ports".
Saving names the units confirmed to move; the server refuses any other held unit with a
409 that lists every conflict, and the page turns that list into the confirmation dialog.
A stale page therefore never takes a unit someone else claimed a moment ago.

Who may move: a group admin, any unit; a desk member, only units on desks they are also
on. A move that would leave the source desk with no units is refused. Until milestone E
wires it, chasing already planned for a moved unit continues under its current campaign;
the dialog says so.

## Roles and visibility

A desk has two roles, manager and member, but for now they do the same thing: anyone
on the desk can see it and change its settings (name, business units, members, roles,
outbound address, branding of the units their grants cover). The role is recorded for
milestone C, where managers approve agent actions. Creating a desk, deleting it, and
moving it to another mailbox stay with the group admin.

Who sees a desk is the desk's live members plus every group admin of its group. Every
desk check also asks whether the person still belongs to the group, through the same
predicate sign-in uses: a scope grant or a live team membership. A member row on its own
never grants anything, so a collector whose grant is revoked loses the desk the same
instant. Milestone D, the desk-scoped inbox, must reuse that check
through `CollectionDeskService.can_view`, never by reading the member table directly.

<Note>
A desk never admits anyone to Spinal. Members are added by email, and the row links to a
user only when that person is already admitted to the group's Auth0 Organization through
the roster, a team, or a grant. If the person is not yet admitted, the row waits as
pending and links at their next sign-in. Invitations that admit outsiders stay on Teams.
</Note>

## Mailboxes & numbers

Everything a group connects lives on one settings page, **Mailboxes & numbers**, under
Organisation (it replaced App Registrations and Outbound channels on 30 September 2026).
It lists the group's shared mailboxes under the Entra app that reads them, says what each
one is used for ("Reads · inbound for Ports credit control", "Reads and sends · sender for
KEZAD credit control"), how far its sync got, and whether it can send. Phone numbers have a
place on the page and a "Coming soon" badge; nothing is built for them.

**Add connection** is one dialog with steps: choose the provider (only Microsoft Entra is
live), paste the app's tenant id, client id and client secret (checked with Microsoft before
anything is saved), then add the shared mailbox with how much history to import. The
"Mailbox added" summary says, on the server's terms, what the mailbox can do: it reads; it
can send once a desk uses it as its sending address, or it cannot send yet and there is a
"Copy request for IT" for the `Mail.Send` grant; and how the import is going. "Create a desk
with it" opens New desk with that mailbox preselected.

**Sending** has two halves, and the second one is easy to miss. The Entra app must hold
`Mail.Send`, and each mailbox must be declared an outreach sender, because Microsoft cannot
be asked per mailbox. Creating a desk declares the mailbox it picks as sender (a group-admin
action, in the same transaction); the desk's Outbound page and the mailbox inspector can
declare one too. A mailbox whose app lacks `Mail.Send` reads "Read only". The first real send
proves the Exchange side; if Microsoft refuses it the mailbox reads "Blocked" and leaves the
sendable registry until an admin re-declares it. Only `Mail.Send` counts as a send
permission; `Mail.ReadWrite` does not send.

**Manage** on the app row shows its ids, lets an admin update the secret, re-check the
permissions Microsoft reports, or disconnect the app. Disconnecting is typed-to-confirm and
says what it breaks: which mailboxes stop syncing, which desks are built on them, and which
campaigns send from them. Reconnecting the same app (same client id) brings its mailboxes
back, with their sender settings reset. Removing one mailbox stops its sync after the current
run and keeps its history; it can be added again and re-reads its history window.

Two honest limits, until a scheduled sync exists: a mailbox pulls when it is added, when
**Sync now** is pressed and when the domain census is confirmed, so the sync column reads
"Last full sync <when>" rather than a live cadence; and a mailbox added while Microsoft is
still granting access says so and waits for **Sync now**.

## Email branding

Letterheads are set through the desk since 30 September 2026: the desk's Email branding
page shows the header and footer image of every business unit the desk chases for, with
the same editor, library and bulk apply the old group-wide page had. Applying a letterhead
from the desk's library dialog offers the desk's units, all ticked, so "the same letterhead
on every unit of this desk" is one click. The old `/settings/branding` page redirects to
the first desk you can see.

Authority stays with the business-unit grant, not the desk. A member whose grants do not
cover a unit sees it on the page as "no access" and cannot change its letterhead; the desk
role never widens what a person may edit. There is no signature or language setting; the
letterhead is header and footer only.

A group with one business unit skips the unit picker on New desk: the unit is the desk's
scope, and if it is already on a desk the page says so and Create stays locked.

## How a group gets its first desk

A desk cannot be created before the group can read and send, so **New desk** opens on a
readiness card until it can (Figma 5270:1693):

<Steps>
  <Step title="Inbound mailbox">
    **Add connection** runs the Mailboxes & numbers dialog: the Entra app (once) and the
    shared mailbox customers write to. The row then shows the address and "Connected", or
    "Waiting for access" while Microsoft is still granting the app its mailbox permissions.
    If every connected mailbox is already a desk, the row says which desk holds each and
    asks for another. If Microsoft rejected the app's secret, the row offers "Update secret".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sending address">
    Derived, never picked here: ready when a connected mailbox can send or can be declared a
    sender (its app holds `Mail.Send`). Otherwise it reads "Read only" with **Recheck**, which
    asks Microsoft again for every app behind the group's mailboxes, and the help band offers
    "Copy request for IT" with the exact grant to ask for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Desk details">
    **Set up desk** unlocks when both rows are ready and opens the form: name, inbound
    mailbox, sending address, business units and members, all compulsory. When there is one
    free mailbox it is preselected, and it is preselected as the sender when it can send. A
    group with one business unit skips the unit picker.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When the mailbox and a sender are both ready on arrival, New desk skips the card and shows
the form.

## Why a desk is not a team

Teams already exist and look similar: group-owned, with members and business-unit
anchors. They are a different thing. A team is a scope grant: its anchors flow into the
member's visible business units and its invitations admit outsiders. A desk grants no
business-unit scope and admits nobody. Reusing the team tables would have leaked desk
units into every member's scope, so the desk tables are siblings that copy the team
tables' tenancy pattern instead: each child row carries `(desk_id, group_id)` pinned to
the desk, and each referenced unit or mailbox is pinned to the same group.
