# internal-docs

Spinal's internal documentation. A Mintlify site: pages are MDX under `fleet/` and the
root, navigation lives in `docs.json`, and `AGENTS.md` carries the Mintlify conventions.

Before writing or editing any page, load the `doc-writing` skill
(`.claude/skills/doc-writing/SKILL.md`) and run its review pass before calling the page
done. The bar it sets: a new engineer reads the page once and can act, and nothing in it
reads as machine-written.
